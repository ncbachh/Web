FitAsFuk Delivery Policy

Your fresh, personalized meals delivered right to your doorstep. Here's everything you need to know about our delivery process, zones, and schedule.

1. Delivery Zones

FitAsFuk currently delivers to all major metropolitan areas and surrounding regions within [Your Country/Specific Regions]. To confirm if we deliver to your address, please enter your zip code during the sign-up process, or contact our customer support team.

2. Delivery Schedule

We deliver fresh meals on a weekly basis to ensure optimal freshness and quality. Your specific delivery day and time window will be confirmed during your order placement and may depend on your location. You will receive a notification (via email or app) with your precise delivery window the day before or morning of your delivery.

  • Standard Delivery Days: Typically Monday-Wednesday, depending on your region.
  • Delivery Time Windows: Usually between [e.g., 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM], but specific windows will be provided.
  • Cut-off Times: To ensure your meals are prepared and shipped fresh, changes to your order or delivery preferences must be made by [e.g., Wednesday 11:59 PM PST] for the following week's delivery.

3. Packaging & Freshness

Your FitAsFuk meals are carefully packaged in insulated boxes with ice packs to maintain optimal temperature during transit. We use eco-friendly and recyclable packaging materials where possible. Upon arrival, please refrigerate your meals immediately to preserve freshness.

Meals are designed to stay fresh in your refrigerator for up to 5-7 days from the delivery date, depending on the meal type. Please refer to the 'best by' date on each meal for specific guidance.

4. Receiving Your Delivery

It is important that someone is available to receive your delivery during the specified window. If you are not home, our delivery team will attempt to leave the package in a safe, shaded location if possible. However, we are not responsible for spoilage or damage due to unattended delivery, especially in warm climates.

For buildings with restricted access, please provide clear delivery instructions (e.g., gate codes, concierge details) in your account profile to ensure smooth delivery.

5. Missed Deliveries & Issues

If you miss a delivery, or if there is an issue with your delivery (e.g., damaged package, missing meal), please contact our customer support team immediately at support@fitasfuk.com or call us at 1-800-FIT-AS-FUK. We will work quickly to resolve the issue to your satisfaction.

Please report any delivery issues within 24 hours of your scheduled delivery time so we can investigate and provide appropriate assistance.

6. Delivery Fees

Delivery fees may vary based on your location and the chosen meal plan. Any applicable delivery fees will be clearly displayed during the checkout process before you confirm your order.

7. Holiday Deliveries

Delivery schedules may be adjusted during major holidays. We will notify you in advance of any changes to your regular delivery schedule due to holidays.

For any further questions regarding our delivery policy, please refer to our FAQ page or Contact Us directly.
