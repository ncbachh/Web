Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about FitAsFuk, our meal plans, technology, or delivery? Find your answers here. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us!

How does FitAsFuk personalize my meals? ▼

Our proprietary AI algorithm analyzes over 200 data points including your health goals (weight loss, muscle gain, balanced eating), dietary preferences, allergies, activity levels, and even your feedback on previous meals. This allows us to craft a meal plan that's uniquely suited to your nutritional needs and taste buds, constantly adapting as your body and goals evolve.

What kind of ingredients do you use? ▼

We are committed to using the freshest, highest-quality ingredients. We prioritize sourcing from local and sustainable farms whenever possible, ensuring your meals are not only delicious and nutritious but also support responsible agricultural practices. For our AI Premium plan, we also incorporate specialty and gourmet ingredients.

How are the meals prepared and delivered? ▼

Our meals are prepared by professional chefs in state-of-the-art facilities, following strict food safety protocols. They are immediately chilled to preserve freshness and nutritional integrity, then packaged in eco-friendly containers. Our smart logistics system optimizes delivery routes for efficiency, ensuring your meals arrive fresh and on time. For detailed delivery information, please visit our Delivery Policy page.

Can I change my meal plan or pause my subscription? ▼

Yes! Our platform is designed for flexibility. You can easily adjust your meal plan, skip a week, or pause your subscription directly through your FitAsFuk mobile app or web dashboard. Changes typically need to be made by a specific cut-off time each week, which is clearly communicated in your account.

What if I have allergies or dietary restrictions? ▼

During your initial sign-up and onboarding process, you can provide detailed information about any allergies or dietary restrictions you have. Our AI algorithm takes these into account when generating your personalized meal plan. We are equipped to handle a wide range of needs, but if you have severe allergies or highly specific requirements, we recommend contacting our customer support team directly to discuss options.
